# a11y.media-captions

A video with sound has no captions track, or a video or audio element starts playing sound by itself.

## Why it matters

Captions are how people who are deaf or hard of hearing follow a video, and how everyone follows it on a train or in a quiet room with the sound off. A video with no captions track excludes them. Media that starts on its own with sound is a separate problem: it plays over a screen reader's speech, which makes the page unusable for blind users until they find and stop it.

## What it checks

- **No captions** (error): a visible `<video>` without the `muted` attribute that has no `<track>` of `kind="captions"` or `kind="subtitles"` with a `src`. A `<track>` without `kind` counts as subtitles, as in HTML. A muted video, such as a silent background loop, has no speech to caption and is skipped.
- **Autoplay with sound** (error): a visible `<video>` or `<audio>` with `autoplay` and without `muted`.

Hidden media (`hidden`, or inside `aria-hidden="true"`) is skipped. Embedded players from other sites (iframes) are not looked into. The check cannot tell whether a caption file is correct or complete, or whether an audio-only file has a transcript.

## How to fix

```html
<!-- before -->
<video src="/talk.mp4" controls autoplay></video>

<!-- after -->
<video src="/talk.mp4" controls>
  <track kind="captions" src="/talk.en.vtt" srclang="en" label="English" default>
</video>
```

Write a WebVTT file for what is said and for sounds that carry meaning. For a decorative video with no sound, add `muted` (with `loop` and `playsinline`, and `autoplay` if needed). There is no automatic edit, because the report cannot create captions.

## Options

None.
