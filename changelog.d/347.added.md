`layout.focus-visible` reports an element that takes keyboard focus but shows no visible focus indicator (WCAG 2.4.7). The rendered check presses Tab through each page in a real browser and compares the computed appearance of the focused element, its ancestors, siblings, descendants and their `::before` and `::after` pseudo-elements against the unfocused baseline, so any change — a ring, a box-shadow, a colour, a shape — passes whatever technique produced it; `outline: none` with a replacement is not a finding. Elements the browser hides from a style read are skipped rather than guessed at: shadow DOM and custom elements, script-only focus, `aria-hidden` or `inert` content (which `a11y.hidden-focusable` already owns), self-animating elements, and pages whose CSS arrives over the network. Runs only under `--rendered`; measures every page at phone, tablet, and desktop width and keeps one element as one finding naming the widths it was seen at. (#347)
