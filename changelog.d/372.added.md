`i18n.switcher`, the third check of the international-sites family, verifies language switchers: a visible `<a>` that carries an `hreflang` attribute — the standard switcher markup — must land on the page the same document declares as its `rel="alternate" hreflang` target for that language. A switcher pointing at the other language's home page, at the current page, outside the built site, or at a page that does not exist is reported as a `warning` at the link's own `href`, with an exact edit when the page declares that language exactly once. Recognition is markup-only — link text and class names are never guessed — and `<link>` entries, `x-default` fallbacks and pages without an alternate set are left to `i18n.hreflang`, `i18n.x-default` and silence.
