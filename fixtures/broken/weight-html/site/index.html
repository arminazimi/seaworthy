Prose tour

The markup of a page is bytes like everything else, and a template that repeats blocks, embeds long inline SVG paths, or ships third-party widgets can creep past a budget a reviewer set deliberately. This page only has paragraphs, and it is already over the line.

Tours of prose like this one are easy to grow: every new section adds lists, dividers, and wrappers, and none of them show up until a check counts them.

  • The head carries the title, the description, and the charset.
  • The body carries the content a reader came for.
  • Nothing here loads a stylesheet, a script, an image, or a font.

That last point is the point of this fixture: the HTML bucket may cross its budget even when a page loads no other asset kind at all.

Fix hints tell an agent what to do: minify the built HTML, remove markup nobody needs, and move long repeated content onto linked pages so no single page carries it all.
